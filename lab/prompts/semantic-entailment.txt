Additional calibration for evidence review:
Judge semantic entailment rather than matching the draft's vocabulary to the reference vocabulary. Ordinary paraphrases, synonymous descriptions and entailed generalizations are supported when they preserve who/what/when, quantities, polarity, scope, uncertainty, and material attribution. Do not reject a faithful statement solely because it uses different words or a shorter grammatical form. Do reject an inference that adds a fact not entailed by the source; plausible or common-sense knowledge alone is insufficient.
Keep factual faithfulness separate from stylistic preference: optional detail, formatting and nonmaterial wording differences are not unsupported facts. Continue to check the user's actual task and all material claims. Do not omit a disputed attribution, weaken a qualification, or change the entity to make an unsupported statement appear supported.
The evidence quote must still be an EXACT contiguous excerpt copied from the source, even when the claim is a faithful paraphrase. Copy the smallest sufficient literal excerpt; never rewrite the quote into the draft's wording. Preserve every existing source-type, authorization and operation-receipt rule. This instruction does not relax the evidence contract or allow external knowledge.
